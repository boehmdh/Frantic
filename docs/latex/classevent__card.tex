Executes the effect of the event card.

This function applies the specific effect of the event card that is played\+:

{\bfseries{DOOMSDAY\+:}}
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item The game immediately ends.
\item Every player receives 50 points.
\end{DoxyItemize}

{\bfseries{EARTHQUAKE\+:}}
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item Every player passes their entire hand of cards to the player on their right.
\end{DoxyItemize}

{\bfseries{FINISH\+\_\+\+LINE\+:}}
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item The game ends immediately.
\item All players count the points of the cards remaining in their hands.
\end{DoxyItemize}

{\bfseries{TIME\+\_\+\+BOMB\+:}}
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item The game lasts for three more turns.
\item The game ends immediately if a player would take a fourth turn.
\item If a player empties their hand before the three-\/turn limit\+:
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item That player loses 10 points.
\item All other players receive a 10-\/point penalty plus points for the cards in their hand.
\end{DoxyItemize}
\item If no player finishes the game within the three-\/turn limit, all points are doubled.
\end{DoxyItemize}

{\bfseries{EXPANSION\+:}}
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item The game ends immediately.
\item All players count the points of the cards remaining in their hands.
\end{DoxyItemize}

{\bfseries{COMMUNISM\+:}}
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item All players are adjusted to have the same number of cards as the player with the most cards.
\item Players with fewer cards draw the necessary number of cards to match the maximum.
\end{DoxyItemize}

{\bfseries{CAPITALISM\+:}}

{\bfseries{THIRD\+\_\+\+TIME\+\_\+\+LUCKY\+:}}
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item Every player draws three cards from the draw pile.
\end{DoxyItemize}

{\bfseries{MEXICAN\+\_\+\+STANDOFF\+:}}
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item Every player discards all cards in their hand.
\item Players then draw three new cards from the draw pile, in turn order.
\end{DoxyItemize}

{\bfseries{ROBIN\+\_\+\+HOOD\+:}}
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item The player with the fewest cards swaps their entire hand with the player who has the most cards.
\end{DoxyItemize}

{\bfseries{MATING\+\_\+\+SEASON\+:}}
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item Every player must discard all sets of number cards in their hand.
\item Sets include pairs, triples, quadruples, etc., regardless of the color of the cards.
\end{DoxyItemize}
